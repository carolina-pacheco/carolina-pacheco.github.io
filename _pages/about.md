I am a biomedical engineer excited about the power of **interdisciplinary work** for the development of effective solutions to real-world medical needs. With an electrical engineering background, my **research interest** lies at the intersection of **machine learning, computer vision, and biomedical sciences**, which I have explored through projects ranging from the cellular scale to full-body motion analysis. From this experience I recognize the **collaboration with domain experts as key** to make meaningful progress in complex biomedical tasks.

I have led and supported research projects in topics as diverse as classification of stem-cell derived cardiomyocytes with potential for **regenerative medicine**, motor imitation assessment to inform **autism diagnosis** in children, action recognition in infants aiming to assist **pediatric rehabilitation**, and multi-class cell detection in **lensless imaging**. One fundamental challenge across these projects, which is arguably representative of medical problems in general, is the **limited availability of annotated data**, which I have addressed through the **incorporation of domain knowledge** in the architectural design and learning strategy of the proposed models. For details please visit the ["Selected Projects"]({{"/portfolio/" | prepend: base_path}}) and ["Publications"]({{"/publications/" | prepend: base_path}}) tabs.
